## Overview

jamilahmed.com is the personal authority site of Jamil Ahmed, SEO & AI Search Consultant, Semrush Ambassador, pharmacist and Founder & CEO of Reinforce Lab Ltd. The site has to read like an industry analyst's desk, not a marketing blog: confident, factual, restrained. It is a sibling of reinforcelab.online (Oswald, IBM Plex, square corners, off-black) and keeps that family resemblance, but swaps the company red for the personal brand's long-standing brass #AF824B. Derived from the existing jamilahmed.com Customizer (Oswald headings, IBM Plex Sans body, brass accent #AF824B, black header) and the LinkedIn banner work, then tightened into one system.

## Colors

- Stone ground #E9E7E3 for reading bands (darkened from #F4F3F1 on 13 Nov 2026), #DFDCD7 for tinted blocks such as the table of contents; white #FFFFFF reserved for form fields.
- Off-black #141312 for ink and for dark full-bleed bands; graphite #1F1D1B for a second dark step.
- Muted #5E5A55 for secondary text; line #CFCAC2 for hairlines.
- On off-black, secondary text is #B3ADA5; form placeholders use #8A847D.
- Brass #AF824B is the only accent. On stone, brass is for large type, rules and button fills only (contrast); small brass text on stone uses #87622F. On off-black, brass text uses #CDA56F.
- Warm palette declared on purpose: brass has been this brand's colour since 2023 and appears on the LinkedIn banner; neutrals all lean warm to match it. Never mix in cool greys.

## Typography

- Headlines: Oswald 500, uppercase for H1/H2, line-height 1.02 to 1.1, letter-spacing 0.01em. Condensed type gives authority without screaming size.
- Body: IBM Plex Sans 400, 18px, line-height 1.65, measure capped near 64ch.
- Data: IBM Plex Mono 500 uppercase 12 to 13px, 0.12em tracking, only for real data (years, dates, follower counts). Never as an eyebrow above a heading.
- Emphasis inside a headline uses brass colour on the same Oswald weight, never a second family.

## Layout

- 1200px content width, generous 112px vertical rhythm between bands on desktop, 64px on mobile.
- Left-aligned, mostly symmetric composition; split hero with portrait on the right. No centered mega-hero.
- Every multi-column row collapses to one column under 768px, image after text.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from band contrast (stone against off-black) and hairlines, not shadows. If a shadow is ever unavoidable, tint it to the ground at low opacity.

## Shapes

All corners 0px: buttons, images, inputs, panels. A 3px brass top rule is the signature mark on list items and stat blocks.

## Components

- Primary button: brass fill, ink text, Plex Mono uppercase. One primary per view.
- Secondary button: 1px outline in the band's text colour.
- Proof strip: real figures (37,000+ LinkedIn followers, 7,600+ newsletter subscribers, 13+ years) set inline in a hairline row, not a hero-metric template.
- Credential list: hairline-separated rows, Oswald title and Plex body.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- Do use only facts from Jamil's LinkedIn, Crunchbase and about.me profiles or the existing sites: 13+ years; Semrush Ambassador; Onalytica Top 3 Business Intelligence Marketing Influencer (2018); jadirectives.com in Onalytica's Top 20 BI marketing blogs; SaleHoo Top 50 eCommerce Online Sellers & Influencers; Fit Small Business Top 8 Ecommerce Influencer (2018); featured in Times Square, New York; 37,000+ LinkedIn followers; 7,600+ newsletter subscribers; Janssen (Johnson & Johnson), Beacon Pharmaceuticals, Square Group; Founder & CEO of Reinforce Lab Ltd; 4 ventures including Reinforce Lab, FarSightX Media Group and JA Directives.
- Do keep one call to action label per intent: "Book a consultation" for consulting, "Subscribe" for the newsletter.
- Do alternate stone and off-black bands to pace long pages.
- Do use the real portrait from the Media Library.

### Don'ts
- Don't use rounded corners anywhere.
- Don't add a second accent colour or any purple, blue or teal.
- Don't use em-dashes in copy.
- Don't invent statistics, client results or testimonials.
- Don't put eyebrow or kicker labels above any heading.
- Don't use monospace as decoration; it is for data only.
- Avoid section numbers (01, 02, 03) unless the order itself matters.
- Never use pure black #000000.
