# Reusable post snippets

Semrush widgets from Impact (affiliate account 1257050). Each snippet shows the real widget on tablet and desktop,
lazy-loaded, and a brand-style call to action on phones (the widgets are fixed at 900 px and break on small screens).
The phone version links to the general Semrush affiliate link `https://semrush.sjv.io/Vm9x7J`.

**One widget per post**, placed after the main comparison or pricing section and before the FAQ.

| Snippet | Widget | Use in posts about |
|---|---|---|
| `semrush-widget-keyword.html` | Find Traffic-Driving Keywords | keyword research, content writing, AI writing tools, small business SEO, SEO basics, glossary |
| `semrush-widget-organic-research.html` | Domain, keyword or organic research | SEO tool comparisons (Semrush vs Ahrefs, Surfer, Diib, BrightLocal), best SEO tools lists, SEO reporting tools, what is SEO |
