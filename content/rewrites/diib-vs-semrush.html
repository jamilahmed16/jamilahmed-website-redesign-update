What Diib does well

Diib is built for people who don't want to become SEO experts. You enter your website, connect Google Analytics and your other accounts, and it gives you a health score with a short list of what to do next. Diib says more than 800,000 businesses use it.

  • Free plan: a site health scan, a daily Health Score, keyword and backlink analysis, competitor benchmarking and a weekly snapshot email.
  • Pro ($29.99/mo): adds diibAssist (ask questions about your site and get answers from your own data), the diibAI Visibility Score with an action plan, daily live help sessions, advanced benchmarking, daily monitoring of up to 30 websites and social media insights.
  • Integrations: Google Analytics, Google Maps, YouTube and Facebook.

The limits: Diib reports on your site; it isn't a research database. You can't dig into any competitor's keywords, pages and backlinks the way you can in Semrush, and serious keyword or content planning will outgrow it.

What Semrush does well

Semrush is a full SEO and AI search platform. Enter any domain or keyword and you get rankings, traffic estimates, backlinks, top pages and the keywords behind them, for your site and every competitor.

  • SEO plan ($139/mo): 5 websites, 500 keywords tracked daily, keyword research and competitor analysis, Position Tracking and Site Audit.
  • Starter ($199/mo): adds AI search: tracking how your brand appears in AI answers, with 50 prompts, plus MCP access to use Semrush data inside AI assistants.
  • Pro+ ($299/mo): 15 websites, 1,500 tracked keywords, historical data, content optimization and multi-location tracking.
  • Extras: the Semrush App Center adds paid apps for ads, brand monitoring and AI content gaps.

The limits: it costs more, and it has a learning curve. A business owner who logs in once a month will use a fraction of it.

I use Semrush every day in my agency work, and I still think Diib is the right answer for some people. Here's how I'd decide:

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Diib better than Semrush?

Not overall. Diib is simpler and cheaper, which makes it better for small business owners who just want monitoring and tips. Semrush is far more powerful for keyword research, competitor analysis and SEO work.

How much does Diib cost?

Diib has a free plan and a Pro plan at $29.99 a month (November 2026).

How much does Semrush cost?

Semrush has a free account with 10 reports a day. Paid plans are SEO at $139/mo, Starter (SEO + AI Search) at $199/mo and Pro+ at $299/mo, with up to 17% off on annual billing.

Does Diib use Google Analytics?

Yes. Diib connects to Google Analytics, Google Maps, YouTube and Facebook and builds its reports and recommendations on that data.

What are the alternatives to Diib?

If you want more depth, Semrush is the main step up. For a simple, free option, Google Search Console and Google Analytics cover the basics of performance monitoring.
