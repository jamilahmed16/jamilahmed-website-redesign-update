What is the Semrush App Center?

The App Center is a marketplace inside your Semrush account. Semrush describes the apps as "carefully selected" to solve specific marketing jobs, from AI search and ads intelligence to social content and mobile app store optimization. Some are built by Semrush, many by partner companies such as CallRail, Crazy Egg and AdCreative.ai.

What makes them different from buying the same tool directly is that they run inside Semrush, on one login and one bill, and some of them use Semrush data alongside their own.

How App Center apps are billed

  • Separate subscriptions. Each app has its own monthly price. Semrush's knowledge base lists apps as stand-alone purchases, sold monthly or yearly, separate from the SEO, AI and other toolkits.
  • Free trials. Most apps in the store show a free trial. LLM Gap Analyzer, for example, is free for 7 days and then $49/mo. Check the trial length on each app's page.
  • Per user. If your team has extra Semrush users, App Center apps are purchased separately for them.
  • Cancel any time. Apps are listed as "Unsubscribe anytime", and you manage them under Subscription Info in your account.

The best Semrush apps by goal

For AI search visibility: LLM Gap Analyzer

This is the app I'd try first in 2026. It breaks down how Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Claude answer a prompt, sentence by sentence, shows which sources are cited and why, and tells you what to add or fix in your own article. The $49 plan includes 30 answer analyses, 30 content gap analyses and 30 existing content analyses a month. If your buyers research in AI tools, it turns "we need to show up in AI answers" into a concrete edit list.

For content and SEO opportunities: SERP Gap Analyzer and Exploding Topics

SERP Gap Analyzer focuses on pages you could win or are slowly losing. Exploding Topics is for spotting demand early, which suits publishers, ecommerce and anyone planning content a quarter ahead. Pick one; at $99 each, few teams need both.

For brand and reputation: Brand Monitoring

At $79/mo it alerts you to mentions, press coverage and new backlinks. For a personal brand or a B2B company doing PR, it's the cheapest way to know when you're being talked about, and unlinked mentions are easy link building wins.

For paid ads: AdClarity and AdCreative.ai

AdClarity shows competitors' display, video and social ads with spend and impression estimates. At $298/mo it only pays off if you manage real ad budgets. AdCreative.ai is the cheaper production side: it generates ad creatives and copy for testing.

For lead tracking: CallRail

If phone calls are your main conversion, as for clinics, law firms and home services, CallRail connects calls back to the ads and keywords that drove them. Voice Assist adds AI call answering on top.

For social and video: AI Social Content Generator

The cheapest app in the store at $35/mo. Useful for small teams that need steady social output; expect to edit the drafts so they sound like you.
