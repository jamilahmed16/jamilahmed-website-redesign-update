What you get with the Lead Generation add-on

This is the part of the old kit most agencies bought it for. It puts your agency on Semrush's Agency Partners platform, where business owners post projects and get matched with agencies.

  • A verified, branded profile on Semrush's domain, with your services, past clients, awards and examples of work. Semrush says setup takes under 15 minutes and verification is done for you.
  • Inbound requests from registered Semrush users only. Requests appear in your account and arrive by email.
  • A "Semrush verified" badge you can use in your own marketing.
  • Profile tracking: a dashboard showing how your profile performs, with tips to improve its visibility.
  • An Agency Score that affects how prominently you're shown. Semrush doesn't publish the formula; a complete profile, client stories and reviews are what it rewards.

The example requests Semrush shows range from $1,000 to $2,500 for short PPC or link building jobs up to $25,000+ for website redesigns, across SEO, web design, content and advertising. Those are examples, not a promise of volume.

Is it worth it? My view as an agency owner

I run Reinforce Lab, an agency that uses Semrush every day, so this is how I'd decide.

  • Pro Report: yes, for almost every agency. At $20 a month it replaces hours of copying numbers into slides. If you send even three client reports a month, it pays for itself.
  • Lead Generation: only if you can sell. $90 a month is cheap compared with paid ads, but the requests are competitive and buyers compare several agencies. It works for agencies with a clear niche, case studies to show and someone who replies within hours. It's a weak fit for a solo freelancer without a portfolio.
  • Skip it if you need a full client portal or CRM. The current add-ons don't include them, so you'd still need a separate tool.

How to set it up

  1. Start on a paid plan. SEO ($139/mo) is enough for reporting; choose Starter ($199/mo) if clients ask about visibility in ChatGPT and AI Overviews.
  2. Add Pro Report and build one master report template: rankings, traffic, AI visibility, and a written summary.
  3. Add Lead Generation once you have two or three client stories you can show.
  4. Complete the Agency Partners profile fully: services, industries, past clients, awards and examples of work.
  5. Reply to every request the same day. Speed matters more than the profile.

FAQ

Is the Semrush Agency Growth Kit discontinued?

Not as a feature set, but as a single product, yes. Its lead generation part is now the Lead Generation add-on ($90/mo) and its white-label reporting is the Pro Report add-on ($20/mo).

How much is the Semrush Agency Growth Kit in 2026?

There's no single price any more. A typical agency setup costs about $249 a month: the SEO plan ($139), Pro Report ($20) and Lead Generation ($90), billed monthly.

Can I white-label Semrush reports without the Growth Kit?

Yes. Branding and white-labeling are part of the Pro Report add-on at $20 a month on any paid plan.

Do leads on Agency Partners come from real businesses?

Semrush says it only accepts requests from registered users, and shows example projects from manufacturing, healthcare, ecommerce and finance companies. Volume depends on your niche, location and Agency Score.

Is it worth it for freelancers?

Pro Report usually is. Lead Generation is better suited to agencies with case studies and capacity to take on new clients quickly.
